Terms & conditions

Last updated July 12, 2026

1. Academic prototype

FullCourt Supply is a student project created to demonstrate a static e-commerce interface. It is not an operating store.

2. Products and pricing

All product names, descriptions, inventory levels, ratings, prices, discounts, and recommendations are fictional sample data.

3. Accounts and payments

The login and checkout screens are simulations. No account is created, no payment is processed, and users should not enter real financial information.

4. Orders and tracking

Order confirmations and tracking updates are demonstrations generated in the browser and do not represent real shipments.

5. Contact

For questions about the project, use the contact form on the About page.
